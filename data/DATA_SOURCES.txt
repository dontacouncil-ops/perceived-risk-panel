DATA SOURCES
=============================================================================
Primary source
  Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, Survey of Household
  Economics and Decisionmaking (SHED), public-use files, 2021 to 2024 waves.
  Dataset DOI: 10.17016/datasets.002
  Download page:
    https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities/shed_data.htm
  Direct wave downloads (CSV):
    .../files/SHED_public_use_data_2021_(CSV).zip
    .../files/SHED_public_use_data_2022_(CSV).zip
    .../files/SHED_public_use_data_2023_(CSV).zip
    .../files/SHED_public_use_data_2024_(CSV).zip

Files in this folder
  SHED_disaster_mitigation_2021_2024.dta
      Pooled analysis dataset, 47,236 respondent-waves, containing the
      original SHED items used in the study and every constructed analysis
      variable (experience, risk_perc, SEW, income_z, assets_z, debt_z, liq,
      cc_bal, owner, age_z), harmonized across waves. Stata format 118.
  by_wave/SHED_2021.dta ... SHED_2024.dta
      The same data split by survey wave.

Provenance note
  The .dta files were harmonized from the four public-use waves listed above.
  The raw-data path in stata/SHED_REPLICATION_FULL.do rebuilds the pooled dataset and all
  constructed variables directly from the raw public CSVs, documenting every
  harmonization decision (screening-gate coding of ND1 in 2023 and 2024,
  reverse-coding of ND2, index construction and standardization).
